Last updated: November 20, 2026

Sites records working hours and reads the phone's location only at check-in and check-out, and notes the time you leave or return to the site while checked in. We never track you continuously, never record where you go, never sell data, and host everything in the EU.

Operator: Sites, Denmark. Privacy contact: sitesapp1708@gmail.com.

We have two roles, depending on whose data it is:

  • Your employer is the controller for your work records. If you use Sites because your employer invited you, your employer decides what is recorded and why. We process that data on their behalf as a processor, under a data processing agreement. Questions about how your employer uses your records should go to them first; we will help them answer.
  • We are the controller for account and service data. This covers the business owner's account, billing, support requests, security logs and anonymous usage statistics.

3. How Sites uses your location

Sites reads your phone's location once, at the moment you tap a button. While you are checked in, it also notes when you leave and return to the site. It never tracks your route.

  1. You tap Check in or Check out. Breaks record only the time, never the location.
  2. The app asks the phone for its current position and accuracy.
  3. Our server checks whether you are inside the work site's radius (for example 50 m).
  4. The time, position and accuracy are saved with that event. Nothing else is saved.

The owner sees whether a check-in was confirmed at the site and, if they open the record, the saved position. They cannot see where you are at any other time.

You control location access in your phone's settings. Sites asks for "While using the app" access only. If you turn it off, check-in by GPS will not work; ask your employer how to record time manually.

Leaving the site. While you are checked in, your phone (not our server) watches a zone of at least 150 m around the site. If you leave it for more than 5 minutes, Sites saves only the time you left and the time you came back. No position, route or destination is sent or stored. You can add a reason (materials run, other site, break, ended day). Leaving never checks you out or stops your time. This needs "Always" location access, which you can refuse or turn off at any time; check-in and check-out still work without it.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see the data held about you, get a copy, correct it, have it deleted or restricted, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and move it to another service.

  • Employees: you can see all your own hours, breaks and notes in the app at any time. For other requests, contact your employer; if you contact us, we will pass the request to them and help them answer. Note that your employer may be required by law to keep time records even if you ask for deletion.
  • Owners and anyone else: email sitesapp1708@gmail.com. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Denmark that is Datatilsynet (datatilsynet.dk); elsewhere, the authority where you live or work.
